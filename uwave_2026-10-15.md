## Proposed procedure for minima finding.
Gather three or more data points near a minimum and fit a quadratic function with the model equation $y(x) = \theta_0 + \theta_1 x + \theta_2 x^2$, and the x-coordinate of the vertex (minimum) is $-\frac{\theta_1}{2\theta_2}$. This does not apply to finding voltage minima with a high VSWR because the minima will be corner points, not locally quadratic. 

Use `scipy.linalg.lstsq(A, y)` in code, $A$ is the matrix whose rows are $[1 x_i x_i^2]$ for each data point. 

Propagation of x-uncertainty into equivalent fitting parameter uncertainty will be analytically possible using sensitivity analysis, because the polynomial regression problem is linear in its model parameters. 

Had I performed Task 1-1 measurements with the free space termination, I would have been able to mitigate the difficulty of locating the wider minima with the quadratic-fit method.

## Correct formula for normalized termination impedance
The formula for reflection coefficient is $\Gamma = \frac{Z_{load} - Z_{0}}{Z_{load} + Z_{0}}$, where $Z_0$ is the characteristic impedance of a theoretically lossless waveguide. So the normalized termination impedance is $\frac{Z_{load}}{Z_0} = \frac{1+\Gamma}{1-\Gamma}$. In my initial scratchwork I used the symbol $Z_0$ to represent the impedance of free space (376.730313412 Ohms), which is the load impedance. This gave me symbol confusion which I took a long time to correct.

Reference: Pozar equation 2.60 (pp. 70). 

## Using the Smith Chart to double-check voltage measurements
> On the Smith chart the position of a voltage minimum corresponds to the minimum impedance point (minimum voltage, maximum current), which is the horizontal axis (zero reactance) to the left of the origin.

Having measured the VSWR and number of fractional wavelengths to the nearest minimum modulo 0.5, the angle of the reflection coefficient at the load can be read by starting the ruler pointing left along the horizontal axis, then rotating by that number number of fractional wavelengths towards the load, then reading the intersecting resistance and reactance curves at the radius matching the measured VSWR on its scale.

Reference: Pozar EXAMPLE 2.4 IMPEDANCE MEASUREMENT WITH A SLOTTED LINE (pp. 70-72).
